Shower or Grower?

Everyone starts with the same 3-letter word. Showers don't change much. Growers end up much bigger than they started.

  • Grow: each new word uses every letter of the last word, plus one. Rearrange them however you like.
  • The target: a secret everyday object with a clue that sounds dirty. (It isn't.) Grow your word all the way into it. Letters you've got light up in the target.
  • Stay on course: pick the wrong letter and the target goes out of reach. You can keep growing, but no happy ending. Throw it Back undoes one step, once. Pull Out ends your game.
  • Girth: rearrange any word in your chain into a different word (same letters) for bonus points. Just type it and press Enter.
  • Reach the target's length and you're a Grower. Barely change and you're a Shower. Lend a Hand shows which letter to slip in; tap it again and it shows you the word. You get 3 per day.

Everyone gets the same start word. A new one drops at midnight UTC.
