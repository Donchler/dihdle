Shower or Grower?

Everyone starts with the same 3-letter word. Showers don't change much from there. Growers end up much bigger than they started.

  • Grow: each new word uses every letter of the last word, plus one. Rearrange them however you like.
  • The target: a secret everyday object with a clue that sounds dirty. (It isn't.) Grow your word all the way into it. Letters you've got light up in the target.
  • Stay on course: pick the wrong letter and the target goes out of reach. You can keep growing, but no happy ending. Pull out undoes one step, once.
  • Girth: rearrange any word in your chain into a different word (same letters) for bonus points. Just type it and press Enter.
  • Reach the target's length and you're a Grower. Barely change and you're a Shower. Help shows which letter to add; tap it again to see the word. You get 3 per day.

Everyone gets the same start word. A new one drops at midnight UTC.
